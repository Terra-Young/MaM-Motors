# MaM Motors: Performance & Sustainability Dashboard

## Project Overview
MaM Motors is a top automotive manufacturer focusing on a mixed portfolio of Diesel, Petrol, and Electric Vehicles (EVs) across major UK areas, including London, Edinburgh, Birmingham, Leeds, and Manchester. This Power BI project analyzes 2,000 recent sales transactions to aid evidence-based decision-making regarding the company's financial viability and changing regulatory environment. 

The dashboard provides stakeholders with a comprehensive view of business health by tracking a total revenue of £482.87M and 14,850 total units sold, alongside environmental sustainability metrics.

**Tools Used:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX
**Skills Demonstrated:** Data Cleaning, Data Modeling, DAX Measure Creation, Advanced Data Visualization, and Business Intelligence Reporting.

## Dashboard Pages & KPIs

### 1. Sales and Revenue (KPI 1)
* **Objective:** This involves the identification of good and poor performance areas by region, type of vehicle, and sales representatives.
* **Key Visuals:** Donut chart for Total Revenue by Vehicle Type, Line chart for Monthly Revenue by Vehicle Type, Bar chart for Revenue Statistics by Region, and a Matrix for Sales Representative Performance.
* **Insights:**
    * Manchester is the best-performing region with a Total Revenue of £103M, while Birmingham underperforms.
    * Petrol vehicles dominate the overall revenue at 44.3% (£213.9M), though all vehicle types showed a revenue decline in March.

### 2. Profit Contribution (KPI 2)
* **Objective:** The analysis of gross profit, net profit margins, and cost structures to identify the most and least financially viable products.
* **Key Visuals:** Clustered column chart comparing Gross vs. Net Profit, Matrix for Profitability and Margin Breakdown, and a combo Line/Column chart for Sales Volume and Net Profit Margin by Model.
* **Insights:**
    * Diesel vehicles, particularly Model C, yield the highest Net Profit Margins at 26.77%.
    * Electric Vehicles (EVs) have the lowest Net Profit Margins at 18.66%.

### 3. Environmental Sustainability (KPI 3)
* **Objective:** Evaluating the carbon footprint of the fleet, fuel efficiency, and the attainment of the carbon reduction goals of the UK Government in 2030.
* **Key Visuals:** Gauge chart tracking Average Fleet Emission against the 2030 target, a Card for Total Emissions Avoided, and a clustered bar chart comparing Average Carbon Output vs. Fuel Efficiency by Model.
* **Insights:**
    * The current average fleet emission is 98.87g/km, which is significantly higher than the 50.00g/km target.
    * MaM must aggressively shift its sales toward EVs to drive the fleet average down and meet the 2030 zero-emission targets.

## Key Business Recommendations
Based on the dashboard analysis, the following actions are recommended for MaM Motors to maximize profitability while complying with future zero-emission vehicle requirements:

1. **Supply Chain Integration for EV Cost Reduction:** To enhance margins on EVs, MaM should form long-term strategic alliances with battery and component suppliers rather than relying on spot-purchasing.
2. **Cross-Subsidization (The Phased Transition):** MaM should ring-fence the high profits from Diesel vehicles to cross-subsidize EV manufacturing expansions and absorb interim margin losses.
3. **Regional Inventory and Logistics Realignment:** Implement a localized inventory policy by concentrating EVs in high-demand, high-revenue centers to decrease outbound logistics overheads and increase EV net profit margins.
